Motive Capture

Select a prop to capture points from Motive.
Wand tip extension (mm)

Distance from the marker line's tip-end to the wand's actual touch tip, measured once with a ruler. Captured positions aren't trustworthy until this is set correctly -- see js/motive/motiveCalibration.js.

Capture at least 2 points (e.g. two corners, or a corner + center) to solve both position and rotation. A single point can only reposition the prop — its current rotation is kept.

Reference tracker (position + height)

Instead of touching a point with the wand: place a 3-marker reference tracker (e.g. the Triangle probe) flat on top of the prop and load its CSV here. Sets X/Y from its centroid and Height from Motive's Y (up-axis) reading — rotation is left as-is.

Camera Motive Capture

Select a camera to capture its position from Motive.

The camera's 3 rig markers (back/left/right) are one rigid body -- load a single Motive CSV capture of the camera at this position and all 3 are captured and solved together.
